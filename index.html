All scores

CRPS and MAE: lower is better. Coverage: share of outcomes inside the 80% interval (target 80%). ECE: average calibration error of over/under probabilities (gate: at most 0.02). Bias: average predicted minus actual.

How this works

  • B1 projects a player from his own recent targets, catch rate and yards per catch, shrunk toward his position's average.
  • B2 projects targets as the player's share of team targets × the team's expected targets, using the spread and total.
  • M1 adds one thing to B2: when teammates are out, the active players' shares are re-scaled so the team's targets still add up.
  • All three simulate each game: targets, then catches, then yards per catch drawn from real NFL catches, then a per-position calibration fit only on earlier seasons.

Honest notes

  • The test plan and pass/fail rule were written and committed before the 2025 season was scored, and 2025 was scored once.
  • An opponent (defense) adjustment was tested and removed: it did not improve 2024 results.
  • A data bug was found and fixed during this phase: 2016 Chargers and 2016–2019 Raiders stats used relocated team codes and didn't link to their games.
  • Watch item: in the first five weeks of 2026 (798 player-games), M1's yards intervals covered 74.7% instead of 80%, and over/under calibration error was 0.029. That is a small sample, but it is the first sign to recheck as the season goes on.
  • Average bias flips by season: M1 under-predicted 2024 receptions by 0.06 per game and over-predicted 2025 by 0.14.
  • Projections for an unplayed week assume each team's actives from its last game. Check injury news before relying on any number.
  • Nothing here is a betting recommendation. Model probabilities have not yet been compared with sportsbook prices (that is Phase 2).
